# About

## Page

MyZine is a small tool for making zines: self-published little magazines, traditionally photocopied and handed out.
Every zine here uses the classic one-sheet format: a single A4 page folded into 8 small pages, with a front cover, six inside pages and a back cover.
It runs entirely in your browser. There is no sign-up, no tracking and no server.

## Story

My name is Istvan Schoffhauzer.

My original vision was to create a platform where writers could come together, somewhere between a social network and a publishing platform, where anyone could publish their own novels and creative writing.

The platform was designed to support collaborative writing and content moderation. For example, in an educational environment, teachers could moderate their students' work, while students could collaborate on group writing projects and share their creations.

Unfortunately, the project proved to be quite challenging, both technically and in terms of its overall scope. As a result, I eventually decided to scale down the original concept and transform MyZine into a platform focused on sharing writing techniques and providing tools and guidance to help people create their own zines.

I hope this gives you a better understanding of the project's background and its original purpose. I also hope that MyZine can be of help to you.
